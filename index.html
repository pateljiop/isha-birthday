You don't have to pretend to be okay every moment. Your feelings deserve patience, too.

Take a slow breath. There is no rush to feel better instantly.

🌙
It's okay to have quiet days.A difficult day doesn't define your whole life.
☕
You can start very small.Drink some water, sit somewhere comfortable, or step outside for a little fresh air.
💌
You are allowed to reach out.A simple “Can we talk for a bit?” to someone you trust is enough. You don't need perfect words.

And if loneliness keeps feeling heavy, talking with someone you trust or a counsellor can help. You deserve support in real life, too. ♡
